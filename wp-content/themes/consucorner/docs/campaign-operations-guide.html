Campaign Management Guide

How to create, check, publish, share, update, and end campaigns that contain both discounted offer products and a mix-and-match bundle.

Campaign structure

Manage campaigns in WordPress Admin → Products → Campaigns. One Campaign controls two connected customer pages.

Offers page

Shows the campaign hero, the automatic bundle banner, and the products selected in Discounted offer products.

Bundle page

Shows the exact bundle builder. Customers choose the required quantity from the separate bundle product pool for one fixed price.

Important: Offer products and bundle pool products are managed separately. A product may appear in either section or both.

Create a campaign

  1. Open Products → Campaigns → Add Campaign.
  2. Enter a clear internal campaign title. Add an excerpt if it should be used as the default banner description.
  3. Keep the campaign as a draft until the configuration checklist is complete.

Configure discounted offer products

  1. Open each WooCommerce product and configure its sale price or product-level offer deal first.
  2. Return to the Campaign and use Discounted offer products to search for and select those products.
  3. Update product selection whenever the campaign assortment changes.
The Campaign does not create discounts. It displays the prices and deals already saved on each product. Confirm every selected product has the intended pricing before publishing.

Configure the bundle

  1. Enable Active (visible on /bundles/).
  2. Set Bundle size (N): the total number of items the customer must choose.
  3. Set Bundle price (P): the fixed total charged for a complete bundle.
  4. Select the eligible products in Product pool.

Example: size 3, price 100 EGP, pool of 12 products means the customer can choose any three eligible items for 100 EGP total.

Configure campaign content

Offers page banner

Banner images are generated automatically from the first available bundle-pool products. Set the optional banner title, subtitle, and button label. The button always opens this Campaign’s exact bundle page.

Offers hero override

Use the badge, title, and description fields only when this Campaign needs different hero content. Blank fields use the global Offers page content.

Status and schedule

  1. Enable Active on Offers page.
  2. Optionally set start and end dates in the site’s WordPress timezone.
  3. Leave both dates empty for an unrestricted schedule.
  4. Publish the Campaign only after completing the checks below.

Slug and public links

Enter one short, unique campaign slug using lowercase words and hyphens. The same slug connects both pages.

/offers/{campaign-slug}/ /bundles/{campaign-slug}/

Save or update the Campaign before copying links from the Campaign links panel.

Pre-publish checklist

  • Offer products are selected and their individual discounts are correct.
  • Bundle is active, size is at least 1, price is greater than 0, and the pool has products.
  • Banner copy is correct and its product images come from the intended bundle pool.
  • Offers-page status is active and the schedule includes the current time.
  • Campaign is published and the slug is unique.
  • Both public links open correctly on desktop and mobile.
  • Add an offer product to cart and confirm its saved product pricing.
  • Build the exact required bundle quantity and confirm the fixed bundle total.

Update or end a campaign

Use the Campaign edit screen to change offer selection, bundle pool, content, or schedule. Avoid changing a shared slug because existing marketing links will stop matching the new URL.

To end a campaign, disable Active on Offers page and disable the Bundle active checkbox, or set an end date. Do not delete the Campaign if reporting or link history may be needed.

Troubleshooting

Campaign unavailable

Confirm it is published, active on the Offers page, inside its schedule, and has a complete active bundle.

No products below the banner

Select products in Discounted offer products and confirm those WooCommerce products are published.

Wrong discount appears

Edit the individual WooCommerce product. Campaigns do not override product pricing.

Wrong banner images

Review the Bundle Details product pool and its order. Banner images never come from the offer-product selection.
